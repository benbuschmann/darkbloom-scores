Method and limits

At least six valid approximately hourly intervals, 90% machine sample coverage, 100 paid jobs, varying activity, correlation ≥ 0.90 and total absolute count error ≤ 15% are required. Agreement is a count-comparison metric, not an ownership probability. Close runner-up machines are marked ambiguous. Missing accounts are not counted as zero; negative counter changes, gaps, resets and partial legacy job counts are excluded.

Only the public top 200 all-time job accounts can be compared. One account can own multiple computers; this version does not search arbitrary machine combinations. Free/self-routed requests, settlement lag, source timing, pseudonym collisions and similar workloads can prevent or confuse matches. A model label is the latest public observation—not an account’s complete model mix. Checkpoints are retained for 14 days; comparisons use at most the latest 24 intervals. No private fleet links are listed.

Known-fleet validation

This owner-supplied reference fleet is used to measure errors—not to declare other accounts matched or apply one fleet’s ratios to everyone.
